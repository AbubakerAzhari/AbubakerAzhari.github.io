01 — Overview

About the project

Arbagi Platform is a comprehensive Android application designed to bring different aspects of community life together in one accessible digital environment.
The platform combines social interaction, cultural engagement, Islamic charitable activities, and practical digital tools to support everyday needs. Rather than focusing on a single purpose, Arbagi offers multiple complementary sections, each serving a distinct objective while contributing to a shared vision.

The goal is to create a conscious, integrated, and interconnected digital community where people can connect, exchange knowledge, preserve cultural values, participate in charitable initiatives, and access useful services through a user-friendly mobile experience.

At a glance: It is considered one of the comprehensive applications with multiple and varied patterns, as it contains several different sections from each other in content and goals, which are rarely found in one application. There are several social, cultural, and charitable sections in the application that aim to build a conscious, interconnected digital community capable of facing challenges by providing many means to meet the requirements of life in a simple digital form.

02 — The Vision & The Challenge

The Vision

To build an inclusive digital ecosystem that strengthens community relationships, encourages positive social participation, promotes cultural awareness, and facilitates charitable engagement.
Arbagi App aims to make technology more meaningful by connecting people with the content, opportunities, and tools that matter in their daily lives.

The Challenge

Digital services are often distributed across multiple applications, requiring users to switch between different platforms to meet different needs.
Arbagi App addresses this challenge through a unified application concept that brings social, cultural, charitable, and practical experiences together.
The key design challenge is to organize these diverse functions into a clear, intuitive experience without overwhelming users or losing the identity of each section.

03 — Objectives & solution

define, measure, then optimise

Arbagi Platform provides a unified mobile experience built around four core pillars:

  • 01

    Social Community

    A digital space designed to encourage communication, interaction, and stronger connections between community members.

  • 02

    Culture and Knowledge

    A space dedicated to cultural content, shared knowledge, heritage, and the development of a more informed community.

  • 03

    Islamic Charity

    A dedicated area focused on Islamic charitable content and initiatives that encourage generosity, solidarity, and community support.

  • 04

    Everyday Digital Tools

    Practical digital resources intended to help users address everyday needs through accessible mobile services.

Together, these pillars create a connected ecosystem in which different activities contribute to a broader community experience.

Arbagi Platform represents a vision of technology built around people and communities. By integrating social, cultural, Islamic charitable, and practical digital experiences, the application aims to make everyday digital engagement more connected, meaningful, and accessible.

Expected Community Impact: Arbagi Platform is designed to support a more connected and engaged digital community by making diverse experiences available through one mobile platform.
Its intended impact includes easier access to community content, greater cultural engagement, more accessible charitable participation, and improved convenience in everyday digital interactions.

06 — User Experience Principles

The application experience should be guided by five principles:

Clarity
Help users understand the purpose of each section.
Simplicity
Make navigation intuitive and reduce unnecessary steps.
Consistency
Maintain a recognizable visual language across the platform.
Accessibility
Present content in a readable, approachable format.
Trust
Communicate charitable initiatives and community information transparently.
